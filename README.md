# Task 5: Sales Data Analysis

## Objective

Analyze Superstore sales data to evaluate business performance through revenue trends, top-selling products, and profit analysis.

## Dataset

Dataset: Superstore Sales Dataset

Source: Kaggle

## Project Workflow

1. Data Loading
2. Data Cleaning
3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
4. Revenue Trend Analysis
5. Top Product Analysis
6. Profit Analysis
7. Business Insights

## Key Findings

### Monthly Revenue Trends
- Revenue fluctuates across different months.
- Seasonal sales patterns can be observed.

### Top Selling Products
- Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier generated the highest sales.
- Technology and office equipment products dominate sales.

### Profit Analysis
- Technology category generated the highest profit.
- Office Supplies contributed significantly to profitability.
- Furniture generated the lowest profit.

## Business Insights

- Technology products are the major revenue and profit drivers.
- Office Supplies maintain strong profitability.
- Revenue trends indicate seasonal demand.
- Furniture category may require optimization strategies.
